Algebra

Algebra is the study of numbers, variables and equations. It helps you solve problems by using mathematical rules and relationships.

💡 Study Tip

When solving an equation, remember to perform the same operation on both sides of the equation to keep it balanced.

Question 1

Solve: 2x + 5 = 17

2x + 5 = 17
2x = 12
x = 6

Question 2

Simplify: 4a + 3a - 2

4a + 3a - 2 = 7a - 2

Question 3

Expand: (x + 4)(x + 2)

(x + 4)(x + 2)
= x² + 2x + 4x + 8
= x² + 6x + 8
