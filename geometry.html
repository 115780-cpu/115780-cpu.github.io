➕ Geometry

Geometry is the branch of mathematics that studies shapes, angles, lines, distances, area, volume, and space. It is used in architecture, engineering, design, construction, and everyday life.

💡 Study Tip

Draw a clear diagram and label all known lengths and angles before solving a problem. A well-labelled diagram makes it much easier to choose the correct formula and avoid mistakes.

📝 Example Question 1

Question:

Find the missing angle in a triangle.

Two angles are 60° and 70°.

Answer

180° − 60° − 70° = 50°

📝 Example Question 2

Question:

Find the area of a rectangle.

Length = 12 cm, Width = 8 cm.

Answer

Area = Length × Width

12 × 8 = 96 cm²

📝 Example Question 3

Question:

A right triangle has sides of 6 cm and 8 cm.

Find the hypotenuse.

Answer

6² + 8² = 36 + 64 = 100

√100 = 10 cm

📝 Example Question 4

Question:

Find the circumference of a circle with a radius of 7 cm.

Answer

C = 2 × π × r

C = 2 × π × 7 ≈ 43.98 cm

📝 Example Question 5

Question:

Find the sum of the interior angles of a hexagon.

Answer

(6 − 2) × 180 = 720°
