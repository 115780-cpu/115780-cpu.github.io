🧬 Biology

Biology is the study of living organisms and life processes. Learn about cells, genetics, ecosystems, plants, animals and the human body.

📚 What You'll Learn

  • Cells and Cell Structure
  • Human Body Systems
  • Genetics and DNA
  • Photosynthesis
  • Evolution
  • Ecosystems and Food Chains
  • Classification of Living Things
  • Microorganisms

💡 Study Tip

Draw labelled diagrams of cells, organs and food chains. Visual learning makes biology much easier to remember.

📝 Example Question 1

Question:

What is the basic unit of life?

Answer

The cell is the basic unit of life.

📝 Example Question 2

Question:

Which organ pumps blood around the body?

Answer

The heart pumps blood around the body.

📝 Example Question 3

Question:

Which process allows plants to make food?

Answer

Photosynthesis allows plants to make food using sunlight, water and carbon dioxide.

📝 Example Question 4

Question:

What carries genetic information?

Answer

DNA carries genetic information in living organisms.

📝 Example Question 5

Question:

Name one producer in a food chain.

Answer

Plants are producers because they make their own food.
