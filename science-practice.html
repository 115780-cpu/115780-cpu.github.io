💡 Study Tip

📝 Question 1 — Biology

What process do plants use to make their own food using sunlight?

Answer: Photosynthesis.

Plants use light energy, carbon dioxide and water to produce glucose and oxygen.

📝 Question 2 — Chemistry

What is the chemical symbol for oxygen?

Answer: O

📝 Question 3 — Physics

What force pulls objects towards Earth?

Answer: Gravity.

📝 Question 4 — Earth Science

What are the three main layers of Earth?

Answer: The crust, mantle and core.

📝 Question 5 — Astronomy

Which planet is known as the Red Planet?

Answer: Mars.

📝 Question 6 — Chemistry

What is the pH of a neutral substance?

Answer: A neutral substance has a pH of 7.
