➕ Trigonometry

Trigonometry is the branch of mathematics that studies the relationships between angles and sides of triangles. It is widely used in engineering, architecture, navigation, astronomy, computer graphics, surveying, and physics.

💡 Study Tip

Remember the mnemonic SOH CAH TOA to choose the correct trigonometric ratio. Always identify the opposite, adjacent, and hypotenuse before solving a problem.

📝 Example Question 1

Question:

A right triangle has an opposite side of 6 cm and a hypotenuse of 10 cm.

Find the value of sin θ.

Answer

sin θ = Opposite ÷ Hypotenuse

sin θ = 6 ÷ 10 = 0.6

📝 Example Question 2

Question:

A right triangle has an adjacent side of 8 cm and a hypotenuse of 10 cm.

Find cos θ.

Answer

cos θ = Adjacent ÷ Hypotenuse

cos θ = 8 ÷ 10 = 0.8

📝 Example Question 3

Question:

A right triangle has an opposite side of 12 cm and an adjacent side of 5 cm.

Find tan θ.

Answer

tan θ = Opposite ÷ Adjacent

tan θ = 12 ÷ 5 = 2.4

📝 Example Question 4

Question:

A ladder is 5 m long and leans against a wall. The base of the ladder is 3 m from the wall.

How high does the ladder reach?

Answer

Using Pythagoras:

Height² = 5² − 3²

Height² = 25 − 9 = 16

Height = 4 m

📝 Example Question 5

Question:

A tree casts a shadow 10 m long. The angle of elevation of the Sun is 45°.

Find the height of the tree.

Answer

tan 45° = Height ÷ 10

1 = Height ÷ 10

Height = 10 m
