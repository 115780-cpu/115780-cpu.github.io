➕ Statistics

Statistics is the branch of mathematics that involves collecting, organizing, analyzing, and interpreting data. It helps people identify patterns and trends in information.

📚 What You'll Learn

  • Collecting Data
  • Tables and Charts
  • Mean, Median and Mode
  • Range and Spread
  • Probability
  • Interpreting Graphs
  • Comparing Data Sets
  • Making Predictions Using Data

💡 Study Tip

Always read the question carefully and check what measure or graph is being asked for. Organizing your data clearly will help you avoid mistakes.

📝 Example Question 1

Question:

Find the mean of the following numbers:

8, 10, 12, 15, 15

Answer

(8 + 10 + 12 + 15 + 15) ÷ 5 = 60 ÷ 5 = 12

📝 Example Question 2

Question:

Find the median of:

4, 7, 9, 11, 15

Answer

The middle value is 9.

📝 Example Question 3

Question:

Find the mode of:

3, 5, 5, 7, 9, 9, 9

Answer

The mode is 9 because it appears most often.

📝 Example Question 4

Question:

Find the range of:

12, 18, 21, 27, 30

Answer

Range = Largest − Smallest

30 − 12 = 18

📝 Example Question 5

Question:

A bag contains 5 red balls and 3 blue balls.

What is the probability of picking a blue ball?

Answer

Total balls = 8

Probability = 3 ÷ 8 = 3/8
