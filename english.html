💡 Study Tip

When analysing a text, always support your ideas with evidence from the text. Explain how the evidence supports your point rather than simply describing what happens.

📝 Example Question 1

Question:

What is a metaphor?

Answer

A metaphor compares two different things by saying that one thing is another, without using "like" or "as".

📝 Example Question 2

Question:

What is the purpose of a topic sentence?

Answer

A topic sentence introduces the main idea of a paragraph and helps the reader understand what the paragraph will be about.

📝 Example Question 3

Question:

What is the difference between a simile and a metaphor?

Answer

A simile compares things using words such as "like" or "as", while a metaphor makes a direct comparison.

📝 Example Question 4

Question:

What is the purpose of a conclusion in an essay?

Answer

A conclusion summarises the main ideas of an essay and gives the reader a final understanding of the writer's argument or ideas.

📝 Example Question 5

Question:

What is personification?

Answer

Personification gives human characteristics, actions or feelings to non-human things.
