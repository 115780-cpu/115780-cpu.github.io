💡 Study Tip

When studying history, create timelines to organise important events. Remember to consider the causes, events and consequences of each historical event.

📝 Example Question 1

Question:

What is a primary historical source?

Answer

A primary source is evidence created during the historical period being studied, such as a diary, photograph, letter or official document.

📝 Example Question 2

Question:

What is a secondary historical source?

Answer

A secondary source is created after the historical event and usually analyses or interprets information from primary sources.

📝 Example Question 3

Question:

What does chronology mean in history?

Answer

Chronology is the arrangement of events in the order in which they happened.

📝 Example Question 4

Question:

Why is it important to consider different perspectives when studying history?

Answer

Different people and groups can experience and interpret the same event differently. Considering multiple perspectives can provide a more complete understanding of the past.

📝 Example Question 5

Question:

What is a historical timeline used for?

Answer

A timeline shows historical events in chronological order, making it easier to understand when events happened and how they relate to each other.

🔎 History Study Tip

Don't just memorise dates. Try to understand the causes, events and consequences of important historical events. This makes it easier to explain historical ideas in your own words.
