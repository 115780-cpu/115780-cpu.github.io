🌌 Astronomy

Astronomy is the study of stars, planets, moons, galaxies and other objects and events in space. It helps us understand how the universe works and how Earth fits into the wider universe.

📚 What You'll Learn

  • The Solar System
  • Planets and moons
  • Stars
  • Galaxies
  • The Moon and its phases
  • Earth's movement in space
  • Gravity in space
  • Space exploration
  • The Universe

💡 Study Tip

Remember the order of the eight planets from the Sun using the mnemonic My Very Educated Mother Just Served Us Noodles. Always remember that the Solar System is part of the much larger Milky Way galaxy.

📝 Example Question 1

Question:

Which planet is closest to the Sun?

Answer: Mercury.

📝 Example Question 2

Question:

What is the largest planet in our Solar System?

Answer: Jupiter.

📝 Example Question 3

Question:

What is the name of the galaxy that contains our Solar System?

Answer: The Milky Way Galaxy.

📝 Example Question 4

Question:

Why does the Moon appear to change shape during the month?

Answer: The Moon appears to change shape because we see different amounts of its sunlit side as it orbits Earth.

📝 Example Question 5

Question:

What force keeps planets in orbit around the Sun?

Answer: Gravity.

📝 Example Question 6

Question:

What is a star?

Answer: A star is a huge ball of hot gas that produces energy and light through nuclear reactions in its core.
