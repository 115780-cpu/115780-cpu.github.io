🛟 Help & Support

Find answers to common questions and learn how to use Study Help Hub effectively.

🚀 Getting Started

New to Study Help Hub? Start by choosing a subject from the sidebar. You can explore learning topics, practise questions and find useful resources.

❓ How do I find a subject?

Use the sidebar on the left side of the website. Select Mathematics, Science, English or History to explore the available learning resources.

❓ How do I ask a question?

Select Ask Question from the sidebar. Choose your subject and topic, then enter your question and submit it.


❓ Where can I find my questions?

Select My Questions from the sidebar. You can view questions you have submitted and check whether they have been answered.


❓ How do bookmarks work?

Bookmarks allow you to save useful learning resources so you can easily find them again later. Open the Bookmarks section from the sidebar to view your saved resources.


❓ How can I change my settings?

Open Settings from the sidebar. You can manage your profile, learning preferences, notifications, appearance and privacy options.


📚 Need Help With Your Studies?

If you are struggling with a topic, try reviewing the relevant subject section and completing some practice questions. You can also ask a question for additional help.

📩 Contact & Feedback
